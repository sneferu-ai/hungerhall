HungerhallPixel — original pixel font (SUBSTITUTE; see IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md)
================================================================================

Copyright 2026 Sneferu Pipeline (Safe AI and Robot Learning Lab).

The glyph artwork in silkscreen_regular.ttf and silkscreen_bold.ttf is
ORIGINAL, generated at build time by tools/build_fonts.py (shipped in this
tree; a 5x7 monospace pixel grid composed programmatically, family name
"HungerhallPixel", honest provenance). These files are NOT the real
Silkscreen typeface by Jason Kottke. They carry the pinned G4 paths so the
real Silkscreen (SIL OFL 1.1) can be dropped in over them later with zero
code changes — every consumer loads res://Fonts/silkscreen_regular.ttf and
res://Fonts/silkscreen_bold.ttf, and the sized resources font_8/16/24/32.tres
+ announcer_font.tres bind them through fallbacks.

Until that drop-in happens, this original face stands in under the same
license terms, and the substitution is declared as a Known Blocker in
IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md at the tree root.
